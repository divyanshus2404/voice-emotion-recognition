How it works

1. Audio is converted to 16 kHz mono, silence is trimmed and the volume is normalised.
2. wav2vec2, a transformer pretrained on 960 hours of speech, turns the sound into features.
3. A classifier on top gives a probability for each emotion.
4. For the timeline, a 2-second window slides across the clip 1 second at a time.
The model was exported from PyTorch to ONNX and compressed to 8-bit, so it runs here with onnxruntime-web, with no server.
